# FAQ

## What is Veridian Motion?

Veridian Health Partners' **flagship AI-assisted, clinician-reviewed
correctional wellness platform** — patient portal (kiosk) plus provider
dashboard, with licensed programs (MSK, Precision Rehab, medical intake, Chess
Wellness). See [Veridian Motion](/veridian-motion/product).

## How are patients enrolled?

By an **admin** — **manually** or via **mass import**. Program seats are
enforced when a patient is enrolled in a licensed program. **JMS** sync is
**planned** — [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## How do patients sign in?

**Patient ID + PIN** (4–12 digits), staff-provisioned. No self sign-up. See
[Patient portal](/veridian-motion/patient-portal).

## Do patients see AI results immediately?

No. Confirmation and safety guidance only until a clinician **approves**. AI
generates a clinician-reviewable plan in the dashboard after processing.

## How do staff sign in?

Local **email + password**; **TOTP MFA** available, **enforced per deployment**.
SSO is **planned** — [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## What staff roles exist?

`pt`, `admin`, `officer`, `warden`, `it_admin`, and `wellness_participant`.
**`officer` / `warden`** are operational (no clinical PHI by default).
`wellness_participant` is for the employee wellness portal. See
[Role permissions](/veridian-motion/role-permissions).

## Does Veridian Motion support multiple facilities?

Yes. Each facility's patients and staff are isolated via **row-level security**
scoped by facility membership. Multi-facility staff can **switch facilities**.
See [Roles and access](/veridian-motion/roles-and-access).

## What programs are available?

MSK Assessment & PT review, [Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab),
[Medical intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake), and
[Chess Wellness](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness) — license-gated and
feature-flagged. See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).

## Is live video, EHR, or API included?

No. **Planned** — [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Is Veridian Motion HIPAA-certified?

**No.** **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** **Not CJIS-authorized or
FedRAMP-authorized.** Agency provides hosting, MDM, network, personnel screening.
Current Lovable builds use **synthetic data only**.
[Security overview](/security/overview).

## Does the app diagnose patients?

No. Non-diagnostic triage and education; clinicians approve clinical materials.
Red flags show **see medical staff** — not diagnosis.
[AI and clinical safety](/veridian-motion/ai-and-clinical-safety).
