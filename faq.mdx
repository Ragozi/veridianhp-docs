# FAQ

## What is Veridian Motion?

A **correctional wellness platform** from Veridian Health Partners. **MSK
Assessment & PT review** is the flagship; licensed programs include Precision
Rehab, Medical Intake, and Chess Wellness. See
[Veridian Motion](/veridian-motion/product).

## What programs are available?

| Program | Docs |
| --- | --- |
| MSK Assessment & PT review | [Patient portal](/veridian-motion/patient-portal) |
| Precision Rehab | [Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab) |
| Medical Intake | [Medical Intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake) |
| Chess Wellness | [Chess Wellness](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness) |

All are **non-diagnostic** and (where clinical) **clinician-reviewed**. Modules
are feature-flagged and **rolling out** — confirm activation for your facility.

## How does program licensing / subscription work?

**Contract-first** (no credit cards). Each facility gets a per-program
**entitlement** (seat cap + contract price). Patients get per-seat
**enrollments**. Access requires both; enrolling past seats is blocked. Monthly
billing snapshots support invoicing. This is **program licensing**, not medical
claims/CPT coding. See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).

## How are patients enrolled?

By an **admin** — manually or via **bulk roster import**. Then enroll into
licensed programs within seat caps. **JMS** sync is **planned**.

## How do patients sign in?

**Patient ID + PIN**, staff-provisioned. **Terms acceptance** is required at
first login. See [Terms & patient consent](/veridian-motion/terms-and-consent).

## Do patients see AI results immediately?

No. Confirmation and safety guidance only until a clinician **approves**.

## What staff roles exist?

`pt`, `admin`, `it_admin`, `officer`, `warden`, `bh_clinician`,
`wellness_participant`. **`warden` / `officer`** are operational (no clinical
PHI by default). `bh_clinician` is reserved — see Behavioral Health below.

## Why isn't Behavioral Health available?

Behavioral Health remains **gated pending legal/clinical clearance** under the
**Illinois WOPR Act**. There is **no public BH product page**. It appears only
as a planned / pending-legal line on the [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Is Veridian Motion HIPAA-certified?

**No.** **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** **Not CJIS-authorized or
FedRAMP-authorized.** Current builds use **synthetic data only**.
[Security overview](/security/overview).

## Does the app diagnose patients?

No. Non-diagnostic triage and education; clinicians approve clinical materials.
[AI and clinical safety](/veridian-motion/ai-and-clinical-safety).

## Is multi-facility supported?

**Rolling out** — facility-scoped RLS and switching when activated. Until then,
assume single-facility. [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).
