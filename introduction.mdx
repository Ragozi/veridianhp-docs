# Veridian Health Partners

**Veridian Health Partners** builds healthcare technology for **correctional
facilities**.

Our **flagship product** is [**Veridian Motion**](/veridian-motion/product) —
an **AI-assisted, clinician-reviewed correctional wellness platform**. Patients
use a locked kiosk; staff use an authenticated dashboard. Clinical authority
stays with licensed clinicians; the product does **not** diagnose. Modules are
**feature-flagged and additive**.

## Why Veridian Motion

Structured MSK triage, self-directed rehab education, medical intake safety
screens, and facility-safe cognitive programs can improve **patient wellbeing**
and help facilities address needs before they escalate — while keeping humans
in the loop.

<Note>
  **SSO, JMS, EHR, partner API, and live video** remain on the
  [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap). Current Lovable builds use **synthetic
  data only**.
</Note>

## Built today (summary)

- **Patient portal:** Patient ID + PIN; MSK intake; medical intake; Precision Rehab; Chess Wellness
- **Provider dashboard:** Enrollment (manual + mass import), programs/licensing, review/approve, messaging with safety screening + Communication Record, admin analytics
- **Multi-facility:** Facility-scoped RLS, facility switching, `officer` / `warden` operational roles (no clinical PHI)
- **Officer & Staff Wellness:** Separate confidential employee portal
- **Security:** RBAC, RLS, hash-chained audit log, field-level PHI encryption (AES-256-GCM), brute-force lockout, TOTP MFA, PWA hardening

## Compliance posture

**HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** **Not CJIS-authorized or
FedRAMP-authorized.** The hosting **agency** provides CJIS/FedRAMP/GovCloud
environment, MDM kiosk lockdown, network controls, and personnel screening.
See [Security overview](/security/overview).
