# Veridian Health Partners

**Veridian Health Partners** builds healthcare technology for **correctional
facilities**. Flagship product:
[**Veridian Motion**](/veridian-motion/product) — a **correctional wellness
platform**. Clinical authority stays with licensed clinicians; the product does
**not** diagnose.

**MSK Assessment & PT review** is the flagship clinical workflow. Licensed
programs include Precision Rehab, Medical Intake, [Veridian Academy](/veridian-motion/academy)
(with [Pure Training Roll — Book I](/veridian-motion/pure-training-roll)), and
[Chess Academy](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness). Modules are **feature-flagged**
and documented as **built / rolling out** — not generally available for real PHI
until compliance migration.

<Note>
  **SSO, JMS, EHR, partner API, and live video** remain
  [planned](/veridian-motion/roadmap). **Multi-facility** is **rolling out**.
  Current Lovable builds use **synthetic data only**.
</Note>

## Built today (summary)

- **Patient portal:** Terms gate, Patient ID + PIN, MSK intake, Medical Intake, Precision Rehab, **Veridian Academy** (44 teaching cards, Pure Training Roll Book I, competency examination and certificate), **Chess Academy** (26-lesson curriculum)
- **Provider dashboard:** Enrollment (manual + bulk import), program licensing/seats, review panels, messaging, analytics
- **Programs & licensing:** Contract-first entitlements and seat caps
- **Security:** RBAC, RLS, hash-chained audit log, field-level PHI encryption, TOTP MFA, PWA hardening; self-hosted fonts for facility networks

## Compliance posture

**HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** **Not CJIS-authorized or
FedRAMP-authorized.** The hosting **agency** provides CJIS/FedRAMP/GovCloud
environment, MDM kiosk lockdown, network controls, and personnel screening.
See [Security overview](/security/overview).
