# Roles and access

## Multi-facility access

Each facility's patients and staff are isolated via **row-level security**
scoped by **facility membership**. Staff granted membership in more than one
facility **switch facilities** in the dashboard before working with that site's
data.

| Access type | Who | What they see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clinical (`pt`)** | Licensed clinicians | Clinical data for **their facility only** (assignments) |
| **Admin (`admin`)** | Facility administrators | Roster, enrollment, programs, analytics (facility-scoped) |
| **Operational (`officer`, `warden`)** | Facility oversight | Counts, workflow activity, safety escalations — **no clinical PHI** by default |
| **IT (`it_admin`)** | Technical admins | Security / compliance tooling |
| **Employee wellness (`wellness_participant`)** | Staff participants | Confidential officer wellness portal only |

## Staff authentication

- **Email + password**
- **TOTP MFA** available; enforcement controlled per deployment (`MFA_ENFORCED`)
- **SSO** remains planned — [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap)

## Patient authentication

- **Patient ID + PIN** (staff-provisioned)
- No personal email required from the patient; synthetic identifiers may be used
  under the hood for auth plumbing
- No self sign-up

## Provisioning

Admins:

- **Grant** roles (`pt`, `admin`, `officer`, `warden`, `it_admin`,
  `wellness_participant`)
- Configure **facility membership** for multi-facility staff
- **Mass-import** staff and patients where enabled
- Assign **program enrollments** within seat caps

See [Role permissions](/veridian-motion/role-permissions) and
[Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).
