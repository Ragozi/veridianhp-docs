# Roles and access

## Staff authentication

- **Email + password**
- **TOTP MFA** available; enforcement controlled per deployment (`MFA_ENFORCED`)
- **SSO** remains planned — [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap)

## Patient authentication

- **Patient ID + PIN** (staff-provisioned)
- **Terms acceptance** required at first login — see
  [Terms & patient consent](/veridian-motion/terms-and-consent)
- No self sign-up

## Staff roles

| Role | Role key | Access summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical therapist** | `pt` | Clinical review for assigned patients (MSK, rehab, medical intake) |
| **Administrator** | `admin` | Roster, licensing/seats, enrollment, analytics, Communication Record |
| **IT admin** | `it_admin` | Security / compliance tooling |
| **Officer** | `officer` | Operational support — **no clinical PHI by default** |
| **Warden** | `warden` | Operational oversight only — **no clinical PHI** |
| **BH clinician** | `bh_clinician` | Reserved — **BH product not publicly available** (WOPR) |
| **Wellness participant** | `wellness_participant` | Officer & Staff Wellness portal only |

Full matrix: [Role permissions](/veridian-motion/role-permissions).

## Multi-facility access (rolling out)

When enabled for a deployment, each facility's patients and staff are isolated
via **RLS** scoped by **facility membership**. Staff granted membership in more
than one facility **switch facilities** before working with that site's data.

| Access type | Who | What they see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clinical (`pt`)** | Licensed clinicians | Clinical data for **their facility only** (assignments) |
| **Admin (`admin`)** | Facility administrators | Roster, enrollment, programs, analytics (facility-scoped) |
| **Operational (`officer`, `warden`)** | Facility oversight | Counts, activity, safety escalations — **no clinical PHI** |
| **IT (`it_admin`)** | Technical admins | Security / compliance tooling |
| **Employee wellness** | `wellness_participant` | Confidential officer wellness portal |

Until multi-facility is confirmed for your site, assume **single-facility**
operation. See [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Provisioning

Admins:

- **Grant** roles listed above
- Configure **facility membership** when multi-facility is enabled
- **Bulk-import** staff and patients where enabled
- Assign **program enrollments** within seat caps

See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) and
[Facility onboarding](/veridian-motion/facility-onboarding).

## Chess instructor assignment

Staff with appropriate policy (`pt` and/or `admin`) can **assign Chess Wellness
activities** to enrolled patients. Engagement metrics appear on
[Admin analytics](/veridian-motion/admin-analytics) — not as individual clinical PHI.
