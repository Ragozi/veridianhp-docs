# Terms & patient consent

Patients accept **Terms & Policies** at **first login** before using the
portal. Acceptance is required, versioned, and timestamped.

<Note>
  Current builds are **pre-production / synthetic-data**. Terms are versioned
  for demo and pilot readiness; counsel finalization is tracked internally and
  must complete before real-PHI use.
</Note>

## First-login acceptance

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public `/terms` page** | Versioned Terms & Policies available **pre-login** |
| **Required checkbox** | Patient must accept before continuing into the portal |
| **Logged acceptance** | Each acceptance stores **document version + timestamp** |
| **Counsel review (internal)** | Admin-only **"Pending counsel review"** status — **never shown to patients** |

## Non-diagnostic disclaimers

Plain-language **non-diagnostic** disclaimers appear throughout the product,
including:

- **Registration / first login**
- **Per-suggestion** surfaces (e.g., AI plan and program guidance context for
  clinicians/patients as applicable)
- **Footer** reminders on patient and staff surfaces

The product **does not diagnose**. Educational and triage content organizes the
patient's own data; licensed clinicians retain review authority for clinical
materials.

## Admin console

Admins manage versioned legal documents via the legal-forms console. See
[Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).
