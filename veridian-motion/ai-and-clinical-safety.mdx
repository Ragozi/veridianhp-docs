# AI and clinical safety

Veridian Motion uses **AI-assisted, non-diagnostic triage** for the flagship
MSK workflow and **rules-based safety screens** in other programs. Nothing
constitutes a **diagnosis**. Clinical materials reach patients only after
**licensed clinician review** where the workflow requires it.

## Design principles

1. **Triage / education support, not diagnosis.** Use *assessment*,
   *observations*, and *clinician-reviewed recommendations* — never *diagnosis*.
2. **Clinician-review gate (MSK).** AI generates a draft exercise plan in the
   provider dashboard. A licensed clinician **reviews and approves** before any
   plan reaches the patient.
3. **Safety gates.** Red-flag patterns route patients toward **in-person sick
   call** / **see medical staff** — never automated emergency dispatch.
4. **Clinical authority.** Clinicians retain full authority to edit, approve, or
   reject AI and program outputs.

<Note>
  AI processing for MSK runs **server-side** — not in the patient portal browser.
  Current builds are **synthetic-data / pre-production**.
</Note>

## MSK AI output (for clinician review)

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Exercise or movement name |
| **Why** | Plain-language rationale (non-diagnostic) |
| **Steps** | How to perform the movement safely |
| **Dosage** | Reps, sets, duration, or frequency guidance |
| **Cautions** | Contraindications or custody-appropriate warnings |

## Precision Rehab safety screen

[Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab) guided self-treatment includes
a **safety screen**. If **any** red-flag item is checked, the session
**stops** and routes the patient toward an **in-person sick call**. Logged
sessions and routine completions appear on the **PT review panel** for human
review. Educational and non-diagnostic.

## Medical Intake red-flag engine

[Medical Intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake) uses a **rules-based** engine to
surface clinician review prompts (e.g., cardiac/cancer/clot/fracture history,
statin/steroid/new medication, severe allergy, recent surgery). Clinicians mark
the profile **reviewed**. Non-diagnostic — organizes the patient's self-reported
data.

## Chess Wellness (non-clinical)

[Chess Wellness](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness) is a **non-clinical cognitive
wellness** activity (patience, focus, problem-solving). It is **not** a clinical
assessment, diagnosis tool, or behavioral-health module.

## Messaging safety screening

Secure messaging may screen for custody/safety concerns (e.g., self-harm
escalation to facility staff; threats/contraband blocked). This is **operational
safety screening** — not psychiatric assessment. See
[Messaging](/veridian-motion/messaging).

## What these systems do not do

- Diagnose conditions or replace clinician judgment
- Send MSK exercise plans to patients without clinician approval
- Provide AI emotional / behavioral-health assessment (not a public product —
  WOPR)
- Initiate external emergency dispatch
- Store clinical content in application logs

## Clinician workflow (MSK)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review red flags first">
    Address any **see medical staff** / red-flag callouts before approving a plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review intake and AI plan">
    Compare AI output to body map, questionnaire, and photos. Edit as clinical
    judgment requires.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review related program data">
    When enabled, review Medical Intake flags and Precision Rehab session logs
    on the patient page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve and release">
    **Approve** the assessment when ready. Approved plan and shared notes become
    visible in **My plan / progress**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limitations

Veridian Motion is **not** FDA-cleared software as a medical device. AI and
rules engines may be wrong, incomplete, or biased. Clinicians and facilities
remain responsible for clinical decisions and patient safety. Do not claim the
product is **clinically proven** or **IRB-approved**.
