# Veridian Academy

**Veridian Academy** is the teaching surface inside the
[patient portal](/veridian-motion/patient-portal), at **`/academy`**. Patients
reach it from the PT tool and the Wellness Hub. It holds clinician-authored
education cards and the [Pure Training Roll — Book I](/veridian-motion/pure-training-roll)
certification curriculum.

Clinical authority stays with licensed clinicians. The product does **not**
diagnose. Card material is **educational and non-diagnostic**; cards carry
explicit **stop** criteria.

<Note>
  **Built / rolling out** on synthetic (pre-production) data. Current Lovable
  builds use **synthetic data only**. Confirm activation for your deployment.
  Academy access rides the existing
  [program entitlement](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) model.
</Note>

## Four tabs

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Course** | [Pure Training Roll — Book I](/veridian-motion/pure-training-roll) |
| **Anatomy** | 19 illustrated muscle cards |
| **Common problems** | 9 condition cards |
| **Mobility Exercise** | 16 exercise cards |

**44** clinician-authored teaching cards in total (Anatomy + Common problems +
Mobility Exercise), filterable by body region: neck, shoulder, mid-back, low
back & pelvis, hip, knee, foot & ankle, arm.

## Teaching cards

Cards are full-page designed artwork; many are two pages. A single **exercise**
card carries:

- Level and category
- Starting position
- A coaching cue
- Photographed steps
- Labelled anatomy
- Dosage
- Regression
- A five-stage progression
- Stop-or-modify criteria
- A common-compensations table
- A **why it matters** section

All card content is authored by **Leon Cherbukhovsky, PT**.

## Access control (RLS)

Card rows are **row-level-security gated**:

- Participants see only rows with `status = 'active'`
- Staff see drafts

## What it is not

- **Not a diagnosis** or treatment prescription
- **Not** a substitute for licensed clinician judgment
- **Not** behavioral-health assessment
- **Not** clinically proven / certified claims
