# Legal terms

Veridian Motion includes **versioned, patient-facing Terms** accepted at
registration, plus **non-diagnostic disclaimers** shown throughout clinical and
wellness flows.

## Patient-facing Terms

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public `/terms` page** | Clean, versioned Terms available **pre-login** |
| **Required acceptance** | Checkbox at registration with **versioned, timestamped** logging |
| **Admin status** | Admin-only **"Pending counsel review"** status — **never shown to end users** |

## Why it exists

Defensible consent tracking and a finished-looking Terms surface for demos and
pilots, with counsel finalization tracked internally until counsel signs off.

## Compliance note

Terms, MSA, and BAA must be **finalized with counsel** before any real-PHI
pilot. Current Lovable builds use **synthetic data only**. See
[Security overview](/security/overview).
