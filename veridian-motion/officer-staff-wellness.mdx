# Officer & Staff Wellness

Veridian Motion includes a **separate confidential officer / staff wellness
portal** that extends a movement-based, clinician-informed approach to
**correctional employee** wellness — injury prevention, movement and mobility,
shift recovery, and general **stress-resilience** self-help content.

Participants use the `wellness_participant` role. This is **employee wellness**,
not patient care.

<Warning>
  Governed by **employee-wellness** frameworks (such as EEOC, ADA, and GINA
  considerations) — a **different regime** than patient HIPAA. Veridian Motion
  is **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified**; this portal does **not** claim HIPAA
  certification or any other compliance certification for staff programs.
</Warning>

## Our approach

| Principle | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Voluntary** | Participation is **optional** for each staff member |
| **Confidential to the participant** | Individual activity is **not** exposed to the facility as identifiable records |
| **Aggregate facility insight only** | The facility may see **anonymous, aggregate** trends — not individual staff scores tied to a person |
| **Non-diagnostic** | Movement guidance, self-tracking, and **referral to human support** — does **not** diagnose or provide therapy through the app |
| **Human support when needed** | Escalation paths point to **people** (EAP, occupational health, supervisors per policy) — not AI clinical or psychological assessment |

## What it includes

- **Injury prevention** and movement hygiene for demanding shift work
- **Mobility and recovery** content aligned with physical-therapy principles
- **Shift-recovery** routines staff can use off duty
- **Stress-resilience** self-help — general wellness education, **not** AI
  emotional assessment or mood screening

## What it explicitly is not

- **Not** patient care or a substitute for the patient portal workflow
- **Not** a psychiatric, psychological, or behavioral-health **assessment**
  product — and **not** an AI that evaluates a person's emotional or mental state
- **Not** a promise of specific health outcomes or dollar savings
- **Not** mandatory monitoring of individual officers by facility leadership

<Note>
  Veridian Health Partners does **not** document unreleased AI modules that
  assess mood, psychology, or emotional state in public docs.
</Note>

## Relationship to the patient platform

| | Patient platform | Officer & Staff Wellness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Population** | Patients in facility care | Correctional **employees** |
| **Regulatory frame** | Patient HIPAA (with BAA) | Employee wellness / HR frameworks |
| **Clinical model** | Clinician-reviewed MSK / program workflows | Movement-based wellness guidance |
| **Facility visibility** | Clinical workflow per policy | **Aggregate / anonymous** insight only |
| **Access** | Patient ID + PIN | `wellness_participant` portal |
