# Facility onboarding & integration

Veridian Motion serves correctional facilities through two **distinct identity
populations**, each handled differently.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Staff">
    **Clinicians, administrators, officers, wardens, and IT admins** are named
    users tied to the facility workforce.

    **Built today:** Local **email + password** with **TOTP MFA** (enforced per
    deployment). Roles include `pt`, `admin`, `officer`, `warden`, `it_admin`,
    `bh_clinician` (reserved — BH not public), and `wellness_participant`.

    **Planned:** SSO via agency IdP (SAML/OIDC). **Rolling out:** multi-facility
    membership and switching. See [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Patients">
    Patients are **not** federated identities. Staff enroll patients for their
    facility (**manual** or **bulk roster import**). Patients accept
    [Terms](/veridian-motion/terms-and-consent) at first login and sign in with
    **Patient ID + PIN** on a locked, **MDM-managed** kiosk.

    **JMS** sync remains **planned**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## License programs (entitlements + seats)

Before patients use a program beyond core MSK (as applicable to your contract):

1. Confirm the facility **program entitlement** (on/off, **seat cap**, contract price)
2. Enroll patients into that program within the seat cap
3. Confirm feature flags for Precision Rehab / Medical Intake / Chess Wellness

See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).

## Provisioning patient accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admin enrolls patients">
    Use **Enroll patient** or **bulk roster import** in the admin console.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive Patient ID + PIN">
    Hand credentials to the patient through the facility's existing channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enroll program seats">
    Assign licensed program enrollments without exceeding purchased seats.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Patient accepts Terms and completes intake">
    First login requires Terms acceptance; then MSK and other enabled programs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Staff access

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `pt` | Clinical review (MSK, rehab, medical intake), messaging |
| `admin` | Enrollment, licensing/seats, roles, Communication Record, analytics |
| `officer` | Operational support — **no clinical PHI by default** |
| `warden` | Operational only — counts, activity, escalations; **no clinical PHI** |
| `it_admin` | Security / compliance administration |
| `bh_clinician` | Reserved — BH product not publicly available (WOPR) |
| `wellness_participant` | Officer & Staff Wellness portal |

## Multi-facility (rolling out)

Facility-scoped RLS, facility switching, and `warden` oversight are **rolling
out**. Until confirmed, assume **single-facility** operation.

## Deployment

### Kiosks

Facility-issued tablets locked via agency **MDM**. Chess/rehab loops are
designed for locked networks where it matters.

### Hosting and compliance

**HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified**; **not CJIS/FedRAMP-authorized**. Current
builds use **synthetic data only**. Real-PHI pilots require compliance migration
— see [Security overview](/security/overview) and [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Onboarding runbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contract, BAA, and data-classification review">
    Execute facility agreement and BAA. Confirm hosting; no real PHI until ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provision facility and program entitlements">
    Configure tenant, licenses, seat caps, and feature flags.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect staff identity">
    Create local staff accounts with TOTP MFA. Assign roles.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enroll patients and program seats">
    Manual or bulk roster import; enroll into licensed programs within caps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provision kiosks">
    Install the PWA via MDM. Issue Patient ID + PIN.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train staff and launch pilot">
    Train clinicians on MSK review, Precision Rehab panel, Medical Intake flags,
    and [AI and clinical safety](/veridian-motion/ai-and-clinical-safety).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor">
    Review audit log, billing snapshots, and admin analytics.
  </Step>
</Steps>
