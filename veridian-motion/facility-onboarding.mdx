# Facility onboarding & integration

Veridian Motion serves correctional facilities through two **distinct identity
populations**, each handled differently.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Staff">
    **Clinicians, administrators, officers, wardens, and IT admins** are named
    users tied to the facility workforce.

    **Built today:** Local **email + password** with **TOTP MFA** (enforced per
    deployment). Roles include `pt`, `admin`, `officer`, `warden`, `it_admin`,
    and `wellness_participant`. **Multi-facility** staff can switch facilities;
    RLS isolates each facility's data.

    **Planned:** Single sign-on via agency identity provider (SAML/OIDC). See
    [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Patients">
    Patients are **not** federated identities. Staff enroll patients for their
    **facility** (manual or **mass import**). Patients sign in with
    **Patient ID + PIN** on a locked, **MDM-managed** kiosk.

    Patient onboarding is **provisioning**, not SSO. **JMS** sync remains
    **planned** — see [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Provisioning patient accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admin enrolls patients">
    An administrator uses **Enroll patient** (one at a time) or **mass import**
    in the admin console to create patient records for this facility.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive Patient ID + PIN">
    The system returns a **Patient ID** and **initial PIN** for staff to hand
    to the patient through the facility's existing credential channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="License programs and enroll seats">
    Confirm [program entitlements](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing)
    (seats + price), then enroll patients into licensed programs within the
    seat cap.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Patient completes intake">
    The patient signs in on the kiosk, accepts
    [versioned Terms](/veridian-motion/legal-terms), and completes enabled
    portal flows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credentials and PINs

- Patients authenticate with **Patient ID** and a **4–12 digit PIN**.
- PINs are **unique per patient**, generated at enrollment and delivered
  through the facility's existing channel.
- Staff may **reset a PIN** from the patient record.
- **Never** use a facility-wide shared PIN.

<Warning>
  **Brute-force lockout** protects both patient and staff logins. Repeated
  failed attempts temporarily lock that identifier.
</Warning>

## Staff access

Staff use **local accounts** with **TOTP MFA**. An **admin** grants roles:

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `pt` | Clinical review, approve plans, notes, messaging (facility-scoped) |
| `admin` | Enrollment, programs/licensing, roles, MFA reset, Communication Record, analytics |
| `officer` | Operational support per policy — **no clinical PHI by default** |
| `warden` | **Operational only** — counts, activity, safety escalations; **no clinical PHI** |
| `it_admin` | Security settings, compliance views, technical administration |
| `wellness_participant` | Officer & Staff Wellness portal |

See [Roles and access](/veridian-motion/roles-and-access) and
[Role permissions](/veridian-motion/role-permissions).

## Programs & licensing

Facilities license programs under contract — **no credit cards**. Admins manage
entitlements, seat caps, enrollments, monthly billing snapshots, and invoice
PDF support. See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).

## Multi-facility isolation

- Each facility's patients and staff are isolated through **RLS** by facility membership
- **`pt`** sees clinical data for **their facility only**
- **`officer` / `warden`** see **operational** metrics — **no clinical PHI**
- Authorized staff can **switch between facilities**

## Deployment

### Kiosks

- **Facility-issued tablets** locked to Veridian Motion via agency **MDM**
- No outside web browsing or external messaging from the kiosk
- Chess engine, puzzles, and rehab loops are designed to work **offline / on
  locked networks** where it matters

### Hosting and compliance

Veridian Motion is **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified**; it is **not
CJIS-authorized or FedRAMP-authorized**. Current Lovable builds use
**synthetic data only**. Real-PHI pilots require owned Supabase + BAA,
compliant hosting, and counsel-finalized agreements — see
[Security overview](/security/overview) and [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

| Requirement | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Signed **BAA** with the covered entity | Agency + Veridian Health Partners |
| **CJIS / FedRAMP / GovCloud** hosting (if required) | Agency |
| Network segregation | Agency |
| Personnel screening | Agency |
| Data classification review | Agency |

## Onboarding runbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contract, BAA, and data-classification review">
    Execute facility agreement and BAA. Confirm hosting decision and that real
    PHI is not used until compliance migration is complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provision facilities and program entitlements">
    Veridian Health Partners provisions facility tenant(s). Admins configure
    program licenses and seat caps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect staff identity">
    Create **local staff accounts** with **TOTP MFA**. Assign roles and
    facility membership.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enroll patients">
    Admins enroll manually or via **mass import**, then enroll patients into
    licensed programs within seat caps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provision kiosks">
    Install the PWA via **MDM**. Issue Patient ID + PIN through facility process.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train staff and launch pilot">
    Train clinicians on review/approve, Precision Rehab review, medical intake
    flags, and [AI and clinical safety](/veridian-motion/ai-and-clinical-safety).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor">
    Review the **hash-chained audit log**, program billing snapshots, and
    [admin analytics](/veridian-motion/admin-analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>
