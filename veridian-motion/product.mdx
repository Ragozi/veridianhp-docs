# Veridian Motion

**Veridian Motion** is Veridian Health Partners' flagship **AI-assisted,
clinician-reviewed correctional wellness platform**. Patients use a locked-down
tablet experience; staff use an authenticated dashboard. The platform is the
**content, data, and education layer** of a broader correctional-wellness vision
(patient programs + [Officer & Staff Wellness](/veridian-motion/officer-staff-wellness)).

<Note>
  **Non-diagnostic.** The product teaches self-care and organizes the patient's
  own data. A **licensed clinician** reviews before clinical materials reach a
  patient. **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** Modules are **feature-flagged
  and additive**.
</Note>

## Why Veridian Motion exists

Correctional facilities manage high volumes of musculoskeletal and wellness
needs with limited clinician time. Veridian Motion gives facilities a
repeatable, safety-gated workflow:

- Patients report problems, complete structured intakes, and use self-directed
  education programs on a facility-controlled kiosk.
- AI and program logic produce **observations and plans for clinician review** —
  never a diagnosis.
- Licensed clinicians retain **clinical authority** and approve what patients see.

## How an MSK case moves through the system

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enroll">
    An **admin** enrolls the patient (manual or mass import) and issues
    **Patient ID + PIN**. Program seats are enforced when applicable.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Intake on kiosk">
    The patient signs in, completes MSK intake (body map, complaint,
    questionnaire, up to four posture photos), and may complete medical intake
    when enabled.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and wait">
    The patient sees **confirmation and safety guidance only**. AI generates a
    clinician-reviewable plan in the dashboard — not on the kiosk before approval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign and review">
    An **admin** assigns a clinician. The clinician reviews red flags, intake,
    and the AI plan; edits and **approves** when ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve and follow up">
    The patient may view the approved plan, use Precision Rehab / Chess when
    enrolled, and message staff in the secure thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who uses it

| Role | Role in Veridian Motion |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical therapist (`pt`)** | Clinical review, approve, notes, messaging, rehab review |
| **Administrator (`admin`)** | Enrollment, programs/licensing, roles, Communication Record, analytics |
| **Officer (`officer`)** | Facility operational support per policy (no clinical PHI by default) |
| **Warden (`warden`)** | Operational oversight only — no clinical PHI |
| **IT admin (`it_admin`)** | Security and compliance administration |
| **Wellness participant (`wellness_participant`)** | Officer / staff wellness portal participant |
| **Patient** | Portal intake, programs, post-approval plan access |

## Officer & Staff Wellness

A **separate confidential** [Officer & Staff Wellness](/veridian-motion/officer-staff-wellness)
portal extends movement-based wellness to **employees** — voluntary,
confidential, non-diagnostic, and under **employee-wellness** rules (not
patient HIPAA). Facility insight is **aggregate / anonymous** only.

## Design principles

- **Non-diagnostic** — clinician review before clinical content reaches patients
- **Safety-gated** — red flags route to in-person sick call
- **Additive + flag-gated** — modules off until cleared
- **Offline where it matters** — chess engine, puzzles, and rehab on locked networks
