# Veridian Motion

**Veridian Motion** is Veridian Health Partners' flagship **correctional wellness
platform**. It delivers multiple **licensed, non-diagnostic, clinician-reviewed
programs** on a locked patient kiosk and an authenticated staff dashboard.

**MSK Assessment & PT review** remains the flagship clinical workflow. Additional
programs — [Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab),
[Medical Intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake), and
[Chess Wellness](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness) — are licensed per facility
and feature-flagged.

<Note>
  **Non-diagnostic.** The product teaches self-care and organizes the patient's
  own data. Licensed clinicians review clinical materials before patients see
  them. **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.** Capabilities below are **built /
  pre-production / rolling out** — not generally available for real PHI until
  compliance migration and facility activation.
</Note>

## Why Veridian Motion exists

Correctional facilities manage high volumes of musculoskeletal and wellness
needs with limited clinician time. Veridian Motion gives facilities a
repeatable, safety-gated workflow across licensed programs:

- Patients complete structured intakes and self-directed education on a
  facility-controlled kiosk after accepting Terms.
- AI and program logic produce **observations and plans for clinician review** —
  never a diagnosis.
- Licensed clinicians retain **clinical authority** and approve what patients see.

## How an MSK case moves through the system

<Steps>
  <Step title="License and enroll">
    Facility holds a program **entitlement**; admin enrolls the patient
    (manual or bulk import) and issues **Patient ID + PIN**, then assigns
    program **seats** within the cap.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Terms and intake">
    Patient accepts **Terms** at first login, then completes MSK intake (body
    map, complaint, questionnaire, up to four posture photos).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and wait">
    Patient sees **confirmation and safety guidance only**. AI generates a
    clinician-reviewable plan in the dashboard — not on the kiosk before approval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign and review">
    Admin assigns a clinician. The clinician reviews red flags, intake, and the
    AI plan; edits and **approves** when ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve and follow up">
    Patient may view the approved plan, use other enrolled programs, and message
    staff in the secure thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who uses it

| Role | Role in Veridian Motion |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical therapist (`pt`)** | Clinical review, approve, notes, messaging, rehab review |
| **Administrator (`admin`)** | Enrollment, licensing/seats, roles, Communication Record, analytics |
| **Officer (`officer`)** | Facility operational support per policy (no clinical PHI by default) |
| **Warden (`warden`)** | Operational oversight only — no clinical PHI (rolling out with multi-facility) |
| **IT admin (`it_admin`)** | Security and compliance administration |
| **BH clinician (`bh_clinician`)** | Reserved for future BH workflows — **BH product not publicly available** (WOPR) |
| **Wellness participant (`wellness_participant`)** | Officer & Staff Wellness portal participant |
| **Patient** | Portal intake, programs, post-approval plan access |

## Officer & Staff Wellness

A separate confidential [Officer & Staff Wellness](/veridian-motion/officer-staff-wellness)
portal extends movement-based wellness to **employees** — voluntary,
confidential, non-diagnostic, under employee-wellness rules (not patient HIPAA).
