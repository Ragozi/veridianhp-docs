# Precision Rehab

**Precision Rehab** is a feature-flagged program that delivers
**self-directed musculoskeletal rehab** based on Leon Cherbukhovsky, PT,
DPT's **Precision Manual Therapy Model**. It is **non-diagnostic**: patients
learn self-care and log their own data; licensed clinicians retain review
authority.

<Note>
  Controlled by the **`PRECISION_REHAB_ENABLED`** feature flag (on by default
  in current builds). Facility access also requires an active
  [program entitlement](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) and patient
  enrollment.
</Note>

## What patients get

| Track | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Course** | Lessons and quick checks → **competency check** → printable **Certificate of Competency** |
| **Guided self-treatment** | The **9-step method**: comparable sign (0–10) → localize → fiber direction → position → contact → dose → retest → load |
| **Safety screen** | If any safety flag is checked, the session **stops** and routes the patient toward an **in-person sick call** |
| **Daily Movement routines** | Four equipment-free correctional programs: Cell Mobility, Morning Reset, Prolonged-Sitting Reset, Evening Recovery |

## Clinician review

On the patient page, clinicians see:

- Logged self-treatment sessions
- Routine completions
- Space for **review notes**

Nothing here replaces licensed clinical judgment or diagnoses a condition.

## Offline capability

Rehab content is designed to work on a facility's **locked network** without
external API calls where it matters for session continuity.

## What it is not

- **Not a diagnosis** or therapy substitute for hands-on care
- **Not** AI emotional or behavioral-health assessment
- **Not** a claim of specific clinical outcomes or dollar savings
