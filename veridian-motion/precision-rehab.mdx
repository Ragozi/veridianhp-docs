# Precision Rehab

**Precision Rehab** is a feature-flagged program for **self-directed
musculoskeletal rehab** based on a licensed PT's method (Leon Cherbukhovsky,
PT, DPT — Precision Manual Therapy Model). It is **educational and
non-diagnostic**; a **PT review panel** surfaces logged activity for human
review.

<Note>
  Controlled by **`PRECISION_REHAB_ENABLED`**. Facility access also requires an
  active [program entitlement](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) and
  patient enrollment. Built / rolling out — confirm activation for your
  deployment.
</Note>

## Course and competency

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Lessons + quick checks** | Short course with quiz-style checks |
| **Competency check** | Confirms understanding before certification |
| **Certificate of Competency** | Printable certificate on successful completion |

## Guided self-treatment (9-step method)

1. Pick a **comparable sign** (0–10)
2. **Localize**
3. **Fiber direction**
4. **Position**
5. **Contact**
6. **Dose**
7. **Retest**
8. **Load**

### Safety screen

If **any** safety / red-flag item is checked, the session **stops** and routes
the patient toward an **in-person sick call**. See
[AI and clinical safety](/veridian-motion/ai-and-clinical-safety).

## Daily Movement routines

Equipment-free correctional programs:

- **Cell Mobility**
- **Morning Reset**
- **Prolonged-Sitting Reset**
- **Evening Recovery**

## PT review panel

On the patient page, clinicians see:

- Logged self-treatment sessions
- Routine completions
- Space for **review notes**

## Offline capability

Rehab content is designed to work on a facility's **locked network** without
external API calls where it matters for session continuity.

## What it is not

- **Not a diagnosis** or therapy substitute for hands-on care
- **Not** AI emotional or behavioral-health assessment
- **Not** clinically proven / IRB-approved claims
