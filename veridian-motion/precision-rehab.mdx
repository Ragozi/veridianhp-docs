# Precision Rehab

**Precision Rehab** is a feature-flagged program for **self-directed
musculoskeletal rehab** based on a licensed PT's method. It is **educational and
non-diagnostic**. A **PT review panel** surfaces session outcomes for human
review.

Patients can also start a [Neck check-in](/veridian-motion/neck-check-in) from
this program (**Start a new assessment**).

<Note>
  Controlled by **`PRECISION_REHAB_ENABLED`**. Facility access also requires an
  active [program entitlement](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) and
  patient enrollment. **Built / rolling out** on synthetic (pre-production)
  data — confirm activation for your deployment.
</Note>

## Course (10 lessons)

The course now begins with a first lesson, **Knowing the anatomical map**
(bony landmarks and the neck muscle map, from the program's clinical lead).
The full course is **10 lessons**, with quick checks and a course check before
guided practice unlocks.

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Lessons + quick checks** | 10 lessons, including **Knowing the anatomical map** |
| **Course check** | Must be passed before guided practice unlocks |
| **Certificate of Competency** | Printable certificate on successful completion (where enabled) |

## Guided practice (end of Course)

**Guided practice** — step-by-step self-release with a before/after **0–10**
rating — sits at the **end of the Course** and **unlocks after the course check
is passed**. There is **no separate Self-rehab tab**.

### After-treatment decision tree (deterministic, server-side, no AI)

| Outcome | System behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Symptom went to **0/10** | Resolved — done for today |
| **Better but not gone** | Flagged for **PT review** (new vs. long-term; keep going or adjust) |
| **No change** | Logged; try a different spot next time |
| **Worse** | A short check separates ordinary post-treatment **soreness** from **warning signs** |

**Warning signs** (any one): numbness; tingling; electrical or shooting pain;
marked swelling or bruising; sharp joint pain; harder to move; fever, chills, or
feeling unwell.

| Worse path | Patient guidance | System behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Soreness only** | Don't repeat today; recheck tomorrow; sick call if worse | Session logged |
| **Any warning sign** | Stop self-treatment; place a sick call | Session **escalated**, written to the **audit log**; patient can send a **one-tap message** to their care team |

## Understand tab

**Understand** provides education **by body part**. **Neck** is live; other
regions are coming soon (Roadmap).

## Posture photos

The posture photo step can be **skipped** when a camera isn't available. The
assessment continues without photos.

## PT review panel

On the patient page, clinicians see:

- Each session's **outcome**
- Any **warning signs**
- A **needs PT decision** cue when the decision tree flags review
- Space for review notes

## Offline capability

Rehab content is designed to work on a facility's **locked network** without
external API calls where it matters for session continuity.

## What it is not

- **Not a diagnosis** or therapy substitute for hands-on care
- **Not** AI emotional or behavioral-health assessment
- **Not** clinically proven / IRB-approved claims
