# Roadmap

For what is **built today** (pre-production / synthetic data), see
[Platform overview](/veridian-motion/overview). This page lists **rolling out**
and **planned** items. Nothing here is **generally available** until Veridian
Health Partners confirms activation for your facility.

<Warning>
  Do not treat roadmap or rolling-out items as production-ready for real PHI
  until compliance migration and facility activation are confirmed.
</Warning>

## Compliance migration (gate to real deployment)

Current Lovable builds use **synthetic data only**. Before any real-PHI pilot:

1. Migrate to **owned Supabase** (HIPAA add-on + BAA) and a **compliant host**
2. Keep message screening **rules-based** (no message text to AI services); move
   any remaining staff-facing AI drafts to BAA-covered or self-hosted
   infrastructure as required
3. Finalize **Terms / MSA / BAA** with counsel
4. Obtain an **IRB determination** when required (program-evaluation framing)
5. Obtain clinical sign-offs for enabled programs

Positioning remains **"HIPAA-aligned"** and **"wellness program"** — never
"certified," "IRB-approved," or "clinically proven."

## Explicitly out of scope (public docs)

- Medical **claims / CPT coding** workflows (program licensing ≠ medical billing)
- AI output shown to patients before clinician review
- Self-service patient or staff sign-up
- External messaging (SMS, email to patients) except allowlisted staff PDF send where configured
- Patient-vs-patient chat or matchmaking
- **Public Behavioral Health / AI emotional-assessment product pages** — WOPR-sensitive; see Planned only
