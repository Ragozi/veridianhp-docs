# Roadmap

For what is **built and live today**, see
[Platform overview](/veridian-motion/overview). This page lists **planned**
enhancements and **pilot readiness** work.

  Do not treat roadmap items as available in production until Veridian Health
  Partners confirms activation for your facility.
</Warning>

## Compliance migration (gate to real deployment)

Current Lovable builds use **synthetic data only**. Before any real-PHI pilot:

1. Migrate to **owned Supabase** (HIPAA add-on + BAA) and a **compliant host**
   (AWS commercial + BAA; GovCloud/CJIS only if a facility requires it)
2. Move the messaging **AI screener** to BAA-covered or self-hosted infrastructure
3. Finalize **Terms / MSA / BAA** with counsel
4. Obtain an **IRB determination** under program-evaluation framing (when required)
5. Obtain clinical sign-offs for enabled programs

Until then, positioning remains **"HIPAA-aligned"** and **"wellness program"** —
never "certified," "IRB-approved," or "clinically proven."

## Explicitly out of scope (public docs)

- Medical **coding / insurance billing** workflows (program seat licensing is separate)
- AI output shown to patients before clinician review
- Self-service patient or staff sign-up
- External messaging (SMS, email to patients) except allowlisted staff PDF send where configured
- Patient-vs-patient chat or matchmaking
- **AI behavioral-health, psychiatric, mood, or emotional-assessment modules**
  that interact with or assess a person psychologically — **not documented** in
  public docs (disabled / pending legal clearance; Illinois WOPR Act sensitivity)
