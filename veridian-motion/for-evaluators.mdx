# For evaluators

For **jail medical directors, physical therapists, IT, and compliance staff**
evaluating Veridian Motion. This page reflects **what is built today**
(pre-production / synthetic data). Treat modules as **rolling out** until
Veridian confirms activation. Planned items are on the
[Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## What Veridian Motion is

A **correctional wellness platform** delivering multiple **licensed,
non-diagnostic, clinician-reviewed programs**:

- **MSK Assessment & PT review** — flagship triage workflow
- **Precision Rehab** — self-directed MSK rehab with safety screen + PT review panel
- **Medical Intake** — one-time profile with rules-based clinician red flags
- **Chess Wellness** — non-clinical cognitive wellness (offline engine; no matchmaking)
- **Programs & licensing** — contract-first entitlements, seat caps, invoice-support
- **Terms & consent** — versioned acceptance at first login
- **Officer & Staff Wellness** — separate confidential employee portal

AI and program logic are **non-diagnostic**. Licensed clinicians approve clinical
materials before patients see them. **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified.**

## Why facilities consider it

Structured MSK triage plus licensed education and wellness programs can improve
**patient wellbeing** and help facilities address needs before they escalate —
while keeping humans in the loop. Do not treat marketing ROI figures as
guarantees; analytics separates **measured** metrics from **estimated** cost
framing.

## How MSK intake works (built today)

<Steps>
  <Step title="License programs and enroll">
    Facility entitlement + patient seats; enroll via manual or **bulk roster
    import** → **Patient ID + PIN**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Terms then portal intake">
    Patient accepts Terms → MSK intake → submit. Sees **confirmation only** —
    no AI plan before approval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clinician reviews and approves">
    Assignment queue → red flags → edit plan → **approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limitations

- **Not diagnostic** — clinician retains authority
- **Not emergency response**
- **Not HIPAA-certified / CJIS / FedRAMP-authorized**
- **Synthetic data** on current Lovable builds
- **Not clinically proven / IRB-approved** claims
- Agency provides hosting, MDM, network, personnel screening
