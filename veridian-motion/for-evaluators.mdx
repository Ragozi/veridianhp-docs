# For evaluators

For **jail medical directors, physical therapists, IT, and compliance staff**
evaluating Veridian Motion. This page reflects **what is built today** only.
Planned items are on the [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## What Veridian Motion is

An **AI-assisted, clinician-reviewed correctional wellness platform**:

- **Patient portal (kiosk)** — MSK intake, medical intake, Precision Rehab,
  Chess Wellness (Patient ID + PIN)
- **Provider dashboard** — roster, programs/licensing, assignment queue,
  review/approve, messaging with **safety screening** and **Communication Record**,
  **admin analytics**
- **Multi-facility isolation** — facility-scoped RLS; `officer` / `warden`
  operational oversight (no clinical PHI); facility switching
- **Officer & Staff Wellness** — separate confidential employee portal

AI and program logic are **non-diagnostic**. A licensed clinician **approves**
before clinical materials reach the patient. Modules are **feature-flagged**.

## How MSK intake works (built today)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admin enrolls patient">
    Manual or **mass import** → **Patient ID + PIN**; program seats enforced
    when applicable.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Patient completes portal intake">
    Body map → complaint → questionnaire → up to four photos → submit. Patient
    sees **confirmation only** — no AI plan before approval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="AI generates clinician-reviewable plan">
    Server-side, non-diagnostic plan appears in the dashboard for review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Admin assigns clinician">
    Submission moves through the **assignment queue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clinician approves">
    Reviews red flags, edits plan, **approves**. Patient sees plan in **My
    plan / progress**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limitations

- **Not diagnostic** — clinician retains authority
- **Not emergency response** — red flags prompt **see medical staff**
- **HIPAA-aligned, not HIPAA-certified**; **not CJIS/FedRAMP-authorized**
- **Synthetic data** on current Lovable builds — no real PHI until migration
- **Agency provides** CJIS/FedRAMP/GovCloud hosting, MDM, network, personnel screening
- **AI can be wrong** — clinician review mandatory
- **No public documentation** of AI behavioral-health / emotional-assessment modules
