# Provider dashboard

The provider dashboard is the staff-facing surface of Veridian Motion.
Licensed clinicians retain **clinical authority** — AI output is triage support
only and does not reach patients until **approved**.

<Note>
  Capabilities below are **built / pre-production / rolling out**. Confirm
  feature flags and entitlements for your facility before assuming a module is
  active.
</Note>

## Sign-in and first login

Staff sign in with **email**, **password**, and **TOTP MFA** (enforced per
deployment). An **admin** grants roles and (when multi-facility is enabled)
facility membership. See [Roles and access](/veridian-motion/roles-and-access).

## Patient roster

A **searchable, filterable patient roster** lists enrolled patients for the
facility. **`warden` / `officer`** users do not receive clinical roster views by
default — operational metrics only.

## Enroll patient and license programs

1. An **admin** uses **Enroll patient** or **bulk roster import** to create records
2. The system returns **Patient ID** and **initial PIN** for staff handoff
3. Admins configure [program entitlements](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing)
   (seats + contract price) and enroll patients within the seat cap
4. The patient accepts Terms and completes intake on the kiosk

**JMS** sync remains **planned** — see [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Assignment queue (MSK)

New MSK submissions appear in an **assignment queue** until an **admin assigns
a clinician**. See [Status and workflow](/veridian-motion/status-and-workflow).

## Assessment review and approve (MSK)

Opening an assessment shows body map, complaint, questionnaire, photos,
AI-generated exercise plan (non-diagnostic), and red-flag callouts. The
clinician **approves** when ready. Until then, draft AI content is not visible
to the patient.

## Program review workflows

### Precision Rehab review panel

On the patient page: each session's **outcome**, any **warning signs**, a
**needs PT decision** cue, guided-practice logs, and clinician **review notes**.
See [Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab).

### Neck check-in review

Completed cervical check-ins appear in a **staff review** view. Clinician-only
reasoning notes stay database-separated from the patient summary. See
[Neck check-in](/veridian-motion/neck-check-in).

### Medical Intake review

Profile plus **highlighted red flags**; clinician marks **reviewed**. See
[Medical Intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake).

### Chess Academy assignment and engagement

Staff with policy permission **assign Chess Academy activities** to enrolled
patients. Engagement metrics appear on
[Admin analytics](/veridian-motion/admin-analytics) (including Usage & activity).
See [Chess Academy](/veridian-motion/chess-wellness).

## Tools

### PDF export

Export a plain assessment **PDF**. Where configured, send to an **allowlisted
email**.

### Secure messaging

Append-only clinician-to-patient messaging with **safety screening**; admins may
access the **Communication Record** (PDF; audit-logged). See
[Messaging](/veridian-motion/messaging).

### Admin analytics

Per-facility, admin-only engagement reporting — **measured** vs clearly labeled
**estimated** figures — plus **Usage & activity** (aggregate only; no individual
PHI).

### Programs & Access

License programs on/off, set seats and price, bulk enroll, export monthly
billing snapshots / invoice-support PDFs.
