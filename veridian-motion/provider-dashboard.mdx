# Provider dashboard

The provider dashboard is the staff-facing surface of Veridian Motion.
Licensed clinicians retain **clinical authority** — AI output is triage support
only and does not reach patients until **approved**.

## Sign-in and first login

Staff sign in with **email**, **password**, and **TOTP MFA** (enforced per
deployment). An **admin** grants roles and facility membership. See
[Roles and access](/veridian-motion/roles-and-access).

## Patient roster

A **searchable, filterable patient roster** lists enrolled patients for the
**selected facility**. Multi-facility staff **switch facilities** before opening
clinical records. **`warden`** / **`officer`** users do not receive clinical
roster views by default — operational metrics only.

## Enroll patient

1. An **admin** uses **Enroll patient** or **mass import** to create records
2. The system returns **Patient ID** and **initial PIN** for staff handoff
3. Admins enroll patients into licensed
   [programs](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) within seat caps
4. The patient completes intake on the kiosk

Staff **reset PINs** from the patient record when needed. **JMS** sync remains
**planned** — see [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap).

## Assignment queue

New MSK submissions appear in an **assignment queue** until an **admin assigns
a clinician**. See [Status and workflow](/veridian-motion/status-and-workflow).

## Assessment review and approve

Opening an assessment shows:

- **Body-map selections**, complaint, and **questionnaire** responses
- **Posture photos** (up to four views)
- **AI-generated exercise plan** — name, why, steps, dosage, cautions — for
  clinician review (non-diagnostic)
- **Red-flag callouts** — **review first**; patients may see calm **see medical
  staff** guidance, never a diagnosis
- **Clinician notes** — mark **shared with patient** only when appropriate
- When enabled: **[Medical intake](/veridian-motion/medical-intake)** profile +
  flags; **[Precision Rehab](/veridian-motion/precision-rehab)** session logs
  and routine completions

The clinician **approves** the assessment when ready. Until then, **nothing**
from AI or the draft plan is visible to the patient.

<Warning>
  Red-flag alerts are **AI-assisted triage support**, not a diagnosis or
  emergency dispatch system.
</Warning>

## Tools (built today)

### PDF export

Export a **plain PDF** of an assessment for records or handoff. Where
configured, send to an **allowlisted email**. Generation occurs server-side.

### Secure messaging

**Clinician-to-patient messaging** — append-only, with **safety screening**
(self-harm escalated to facility staff in real time; threats/contraband
blocked). Admins may access the immutable **Communication Record** (PDF export;
every access audit-logged). See [Messaging](/veridian-motion/messaging).

### Admin analytics

Per-facility, **admin-only** engagement and outcomes dashboard — **measured**
vs clearly labeled **estimated** cost figures; no individual PHI. See
[Admin analytics](/veridian-motion/admin-analytics).

### Programs & Access

License programs on/off, set seats and price, run the pricing calculator, bulk
enroll, and export monthly billing snapshots / invoice PDFs. See
[Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).
