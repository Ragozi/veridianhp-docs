# Medical intake

**Medical intake** is a feature-flagged, one-time **medical profile** patients
complete on the portal. A server-side **red-flag engine** highlights risk
signals for clinician review. The profile is **non-diagnostic** and includes an
exact non-diagnostic attestation.

<Note>
  Controlled by the **`MEDICAL_INTAKE_ENABLED`** feature flag (on by default in
  current builds). Clinical model credit: **Dominic Marchiafava, PA-C**.
</Note>

## What patients complete

| Section | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **History** | Relevant medical history items |
| **Medications** | Current meds plus a **side-effect screen** |
| **Allergies** | Including severe allergy flags |
| **Surgical** | Recent surgery indicators |
| **Social** | Activity and sleep context |

**Emergency contact is omitted** for facility security reasons.

## Red-flag engine

After submission, the platform raises clinician-facing triggers such as:

- Cardiac / cancer / clot / fracture history signals
- Statin / steroid / new-medication signals
- Severe allergy
- Recent surgery
- Activity / sleep concerns

Clinicians see the **profile plus highlighted flags** on the patient page and
mark the intake **reviewed**.

## What it is not

- **Not a diagnosis** or treatment plan
- **Not** a full EHR chart
- **Not** behavioral-health or psychological assessment
- **Not** a substitute for facility sick-call protocols

## Why it exists

A Layer-1 medical safety screen that complements MSK assessment and Precision
Rehab — organizing the patient's own reported data for human clinician review.
