# Chess Wellness

**Chess Wellness** is a **subscribable cognitive-wellness program** —
play, puzzles, lessons, and solo competition — licensed **per facility** and
**per patient**. It is designed for locked facility networks: the engine and
puzzles run **on-device** with **no chess API** and **no external matchmaking**.

<Note>
  Controlled by the **`CHESS_ENABLED`** feature flag (on by default in current
  builds). Facility access requires an active
  [program entitlement](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing) and patient
  enrollment with available seats.
</Note>

## What patients get

| Area | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Vs a bundled on-device engine (3 levels); save / resume; **offline** |
| **Puzzles** | 36 tactics puzzles; each proven a unique mate |
| **Challenges** | Daily puzzle (streaks), Puzzle Rush (timed), Ladder |
| **Ranking & seasons** | Personal rating, tiers (Bronze→Platinum), season points, streaks — **solo only**, no patient-vs-patient |
| **Chess Academy** | Curriculum from **Dr. Mikhail Korenman's** handbook — lessons across 4 units, checkmate drills vs the engine, **Certificate of Completion**, interactive teaching games |
| **Instructor assignment** | Staff can assign specific activities to a patient |

## Security design (corrections)

- **No chat** and **no matchmaking** between patients
- Engine and puzzle content run **without outbound chess-API calls**
- Rules via **chess.js** (BSD); Veridian's bundled engine avoids GPL constraints.
  Stronger play via Stockfish is a **documented later upgrade** (separate process,
  GPLv3 compliance) if needed — see [Roadmap](/veridian-motion/roadmap)

## Licensing

`hasProgramAccess(patient, program)` requires:

1. The facility holds an **active entitlement**, and
2. The patient holds an **active enrollment** within the purchased **seat cap**

See [Programs & licensing](/veridian-motion/programs-and-licensing).

## Engagement metrics

Chess engagement appears on the **admin analytics** dashboard at the facility /
program level — not as individual clinical PHI.

## Why it exists

Patience, focus, and cognitive engagement — a facility-safe, offline program
with curriculum from a titled chess authority.
